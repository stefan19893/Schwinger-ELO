# Phase 4 — ELO engine (Milestone 3b)
**Agent:** elo-modeler

## Goal
`SchwingElo` engine implementing spec §4.2 with full rating history.

## Exit criteria
- `src/pipeline/elo_engine.py`: expected score, draws (S=0.5), MoV multiplier, festival K-factors, April mean reversion (δ≈0.10, mean 1500), provisional flag after >1.5 inactive seasons.
- Bouts processed in chronological order (date, then gang_nr); deterministic output.
- `alpha` and `BaselineDiff` chosen with documented reasoning in `STATE.md` Decisions.
- `tests/test_elo.py`: unit tests for each rule + sanity tests on real data (elite athletes such as Glarner, Wicki, Reichmuth, Forrer rank near the top in their peak seasons).
- Parameters live in `src/config.py`.
- `python -m src.cli elo` writes `data/processed/ratings.parquet` (athlete, date, fest_id, rating_before, rating_after); works with `--sample`.

- 2026-10-03 — **Task 5 done.** New `src/pipeline/elo_eval.py` (evaluation only, never changes ratings) and `python -m src.cli elo --evaluate` (prints the whole evidence report, ~1 min on the real data, also runs on `--sample`). Metrics: Brier `mean((S-E)^2)` and log-loss with fractional scores on the pre-bout expected scores; parameters chosen on seasons 2013-2019 ("train"), checked on 2021-2026 ("test"); `E = 0.5` everywhere gives Brier 0.20 / log-loss 0.693. Results on the real data (491,597 bouts), all run, nothing inferred:
  - **Update order** (no MoV; train / test Brier of each mode's own predictions, then of pre-festival predictions): festival 0.13057 / 0.12546 (same), phase 0.13036 / 0.12515 and 0.13061 / 0.12552, gang = sequential 0.13047 / 0.12524 and 0.13056 / 0.12555. Differences are in the 4th decimal. Gang-noise test (every bout flagged `gang_collision` / `gang_uncertain` / `gang_inferred` moved one Gang up or down): festival 0 by construction; sequential mean 0.03, p99 0.34, max 3.1 rating points; gang max 3.8; phase max 10.9. **Chosen: `festival`** (equal accuracy, exactly invariant). Caveat: with K-factors three times as large the sequential order is clearly better (test Brier 0.1124 vs 0.1146), so revisit the mode if K is ever raised.
  - **MoV:** graded wins have a margin of 1.00 (5.6 %), 1.25 (45.4 %) or 1.50 (49.0 %), mean 1.3586 (1.35-1.36 in every season). With a baseline below the mean every alpha "helps" only because the mean multiplier exceeds 1, i.e. K grows (baseline 1.00, alpha 2: mean lambda 1.72, test Brier 0.1166; the Brier is a monotone function of the mean lambda). With the K-neutral baseline 1.36 the gain is real but small: alpha 0 / 0.25 / 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 -> train Brier 0.13057 / 0.13039 / 0.13023 / 0.12996 / 0.12974 / 0.12950, test 0.12546 / 0.12531 / 0.12517 / 0.12495 / 0.12478 / 0.12458 (log-loss test 0.53171 -> 0.53045 at alpha 1); a negative alpha is worse (-0.5: 0.13098 / 0.12582). **Chosen: `mov_alpha = 1.0`, `mov_baseline_diff = 1.36`** -> lambda 0.64 (9.75 : 8.75), 0.89 (margin 1.25), 1.14 (10 : 8.50); improvement 0.4-0.5 % of the Brier score — the MoV term adds little. No interior optimum: the gain keeps growing slowly until the clamp at 0.5 starts to bite (alpha > 1.39), so alpha = 1 is a judgement call, not a fitted optimum.
  - **K and delta are kept as specified, but they are far from the predictive optimum** (reference scan, MoV on): K x 0.5 / 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 6 -> see `elo --evaluate`; without MoV: K x 1 test Brier 0.12546, x 2 0.11509, x 3 0.11218, x 4 0.11227, x 6 0.11711; one flat K for all categories: 48 -> 0.1137, 64 -> 0.1115, 96 -> 0.1115 (2013+), i.e. the tiering itself does not help prediction, the level does. delta 0 / 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.20 -> test Brier 0.12036 / 0.12265 / 0.12546 / 0.13141. Season start month 1 / 4 / 11: 0.12816 / 0.12829 / 0.12817 (2013+, irrelevant).
  - **Calibration: the model is under-confident.** Favourite by 100-150 points: predicted 0.671, observed 0.764; 200-300: 0.801 vs 0.912; 300-400: 0.876 vs 0.960. The observed scores correspond to gaps about twice as large (implied gap 204 / 407 / 553). Ratings are compressed by low K + 10 % reversion + newcomers at 1500.
  - **Experience / provisional threshold** (2013+, Brier by career bouts of the less experienced athlete before the festival): 0 bouts 0.2435 (worse than a coin flip: newcomers at 1500 are overrated — they end their first season at 1410-1455 on average), 1-5 0.234, 6-11 0.205, 12-17 0.176, 18-23 0.155, 24-35 0.137, 36-59 0.120, 60-119 0.111, 120+ 0.105. **Chosen: `provisional_min_bouts = 24`** (four festivals; below it the error is above the all-bout average of 0.128 by >= 20 %). 2,351 athletes are below the threshold (all 1,501 one-festival athletes among them). `--sample` uses 6 (five festivals only) unless set explicitly.
  - **Burn-in:** Brier per season 2011 0.179, 2012 0.153, 2013 0.139, 2014 0.137, 2015 0.134, 2016+ 0.119-0.130 (2021 0.139 after the Covid gap). **Chosen: `elo_first_ranked_season = 2012`** (2011 rated, not ranked, not used for evaluation; evaluation starts 2013). The top of the scale needs longer: mean of the 20 best season-end ratings 1841 (2011), 1976, 2073, 2133, 2153, 2207 (2016), then 2200-2258 — peak ratings before 2016 are not comparable with later ones.
  - **Drift:** mean of all rated athletes is exactly 1500 at all times (zero-sum, reversion toward 1500). Athletes active in a season: mean 1500 (2011) -> 1524 (2016) -> 1536 (2019), then flat 1532-1538; sd 70 -> 182 -> 202, then 184-202; no inflation after 2019.
  - **Identity sensitivity** (bouts of rows with confidence <= 0.4 removed: 6,313 bouts): all top-100 ratings shift by -1.0 to -1.5 points (uniformly), largest rank change in the top 100 is 1, top 20 identical; 17 of 2,020 ranked athletes move by more than 10 points (max 161, the uncertain identities themselves). Marker: 307 athletes `identity_uncertain` (>= 10 low-confidence rows or >= 25 %; includes the 122 `not_a_name`), 14 of them currently ranked, the best at rank 97 (`burch-jonas-p907`).
  - Config now: `elo_update_mode="festival"`, `mov_alpha=1.0`, `mov_baseline_diff=1.36`, `provisional_min_bouts=24`, `elo_first_ranked_season=2012`. 833 tests green.

## Known inputs from Phase 2 review
Notes only (2026-10-01, Phase 2 review fixes; data = `data/schwingen.db`, parser v2).
- **Gang order is approximate.** `gang_collision` (an athlete has two bouts in the same Gang) affects ~2 % of bouts in every era (10,605 of 491,669), plus `gang_uncertain` 4,845 / `gang_inferred` 1,446, because the sources list some bouts out of order (Corcelles 2024: Pellaton has Burger as his 4th bout, Burger has Pellaton as his 3rd). Recommendation: update once per festival from pre-festival ratings, or at most by Ausstich phase (Gänge 1–4, then 5+), not strictly sequentially by `gang_nr`.
- **Bouts with a NULL grade are outcome-only** (user decisions 2026-10-01; parser v3: 730 `extra_bout`, 16 `one_sided` Schlussgang bouts built from the winner's entry because the sheet omits the loser's line, 2 `grade_missing`; 738 bouts with a NULL grade): use the outcome, skip the MoV multiplier (λ = 1, SPEC §4.2). Their `gang_nr` is the opponent's position (often the last Gang); extra bouts are excluded from `gang_collision`. Several are the Schlussgang itself (the Schlussgang loser's line printed as a surplus 0.00 / no-grade entry).
- **A is always the better-ranked athlete on the sheet** (lower sheet index; WIN_A 69.6 %, WIN_B 10.1 %, DRAW 20.3 %): do not treat A/B asymmetrically (no home advantage, no ordering prior).
- **Coverage:** ESAF 2013 is ~70 % complete (640 bouts vs ~905–919 at other ESAFs; the final sheet and the fetched "nach 4 Gängen" interim sheet both omit the 77 athletes eliminated after Gang 4); 2011–2014 coverage is weaker (paired entries 95–97.6 % vs ≥ 98.5 % later; several truncated sheets list only the top ranks; 2011 has only 3 Regional festivals on schlussgang). `festival_parse.n_gaenge` gives each festival's derived Gang count (5-Gang festivals exist).
- `bouts.schlussgang` is NULL (unknown) except on the 19 sheets that mark it — don't use it.
- Only festivals with `festivals.elo_eligible = 1` count (excludes `team`, `ausland`; user decision 2026-10-01).
